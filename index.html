About MY CONSULTANT SERVICE

MY CONSULTANT SERVICE is a career and job consulting service for people who want clear, practical direction in their working life. We work with freshers, students, graduates, working professionals and people planning a career change.

We listen to your situation, explain your options in simple language and help you take the next step, whether that is a stronger resume, a better job search or a more confident interview.

We do not promise jobs or placements. We help you prepare, search and apply with more clarity and confidence.
